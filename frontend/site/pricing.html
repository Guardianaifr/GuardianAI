Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when the 10-day Cortex trial expires?
Your trial expires automatically — no charge, no action needed. All events recorded during the trial remain readable forever; only new recording stops. You can upgrade to Pro at any time to continue recording and unlock on-chain anchoring.
Why is Monad the primary chain?
Monad delivers 10,000 TPS with sub-second finality and EVM compatibility at approximately $0.0001 per transaction — making high-frequency Merkle anchoring economically viable for the first time. We anchor on Monad first, then Base and Ethereum optionally for additional credibility layers.
What is privacy mode and how does it protect my data?
By default Cortex stores only SHA-256 hashes of prompts, outputs, and reasoning text — so you get cryptographic proof that a decision happened without exposing sensitive data. Pro plans add the option to store full text for internal audit use, encrypted at rest.
What is an insurance evidence certificate?
A signed JSON package containing total decisions recorded, anomalies detected, policy violations, all on-chain Merkle root references, and the agent's trust score for the period. Designed to be attached directly to insurance policy applications or regulatory submissions.
Can I run GuardianAI in my own infrastructure?
Yes. The Enterprise plan includes private cloud (VPC-isolated), on-premise, or air-gapped deployments. The Cortex engine uses SQLite by default, making it trivial to run alongside any existing stack without external dependencies.

Compliance language is evidence-based: GuardianAI generates mappings and audit artifacts, but formal SOC 2, ISO 27001, EU AI Act, or external certifications depend on your organization, deployment, and auditor review.
